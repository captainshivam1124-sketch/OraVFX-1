Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information ORA VFX may handle when you visit this website or contact us about a project, how we use it, and how you can control optional advertising measurement.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

AT A GLANCE

  • Project enquiries are handled through direct contact channels such as email and WhatsApp.
  • Meta Pixel is optional and is not initialized unless you select “Allow optional tracking”.
  • You can change your tracking choice later using “Cookie preferences”.
  • Privacy questions can be sent to info@oravfx.com.

1. Who we are

“ORA VFX”, “we”, “us”, and “our” refer to the operator of the ORA VFX website at oravfx.com. This policy applies to information handled through this website and through project enquiries that you choose to send to us.

2. Information we may handle

Information you provide: When you contact us by email or WhatsApp, you may choose to share your name, contact details, business or project information, budget or timing details, and any files or references you include in your message. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is genuinely needed for your enquiry.

Basic technical information: When a website is requested, the hosting provider may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as an IP address, requested page, request time, browser or device details, and security logs. The exact information and retention period can depend on the hosting provider and its configuration.

Optional advertising measurement: If you give permission through the cookie notice, Meta Pixel may receive information about your visit as described in Section 4.

The current website directs project enquiries to email and WhatsApp rather than submitting them through a website enquiry form.

3. How we use information

We may use information to respond to your questions, discuss or deliver a requested project or service, prepare a quotation, keep relevant business records, maintain and secure the website, prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal requirements. Optional Meta Pixel information is used to measure page visits and understand the performance of advertising.

We aim to use information only for purposes relevant to the interaction and to keep it for no longer than reasonably necessary, subject to business, security, and legal needs.

OPTIONAL TRACKING

4. Meta Pixel and advertising measurement

This website can use Meta Pixel, associated with Pixel ID 1100194509661942, to measure page views and assess advertising performance. In the current integration, the event sent by this website is PageView; it does not send Meta conversion events.

Meta Pixel is only initialized after you choose “Allow optional tracking”. If you reject optional tracking or have not made a choice, this website's consent script does not initialize the Pixel. After permission is granted, Meta may receive information such as the page visited, referring page, browser or device information, IP address, and identifiers or cookies that support measurement. Meta handles information it receives under its own terms and policies.

You can change or withdraw your choice through the “Cookie preferences” control displayed on the website. Changing your choice stops this website from continuing optional Pixel use after the choice is processed; it cannot retrieve information Meta may already have received. You can learn more in the Meta Privacy Policy.

5. Cookies and preference storage

The consent control stores your Meta Pixel choice in your browser so the website can remember whether you allowed or rejected optional tracking. The website's theme control may also use browser storage to remember your display preference. These local preferences are separate from the optional advertising measurement decision.

You can revisit “Cookie preferences” at any time to change your Meta Pixel choice. Browser settings can also be used to clear site data, but clearing it may cause the consent notice to appear again. Blocking all browser storage may prevent a choice from being remembered between visits.

6. When information may be shared

We do not sell personal contact details. Information may be handled by service providers needed to host or protect the website, and by the email or messaging platform you choose when contacting us. If you allow Meta Pixel, technical and visit information may be sent to Meta for the measurement purpose described above. We may also disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect the website, our users, or our legal rights.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy notices and terms. Their processing, security practices, and retention periods are not controlled entirely by ORA VFX.

7. How long information is kept

We keep project correspondence and related records for as long as reasonably needed to respond to an enquiry, provide a service, maintain appropriate business records, resolve a dispute, or meet a legal obligation. Retention of information collected by Meta through its tools is also governed by Meta's systems and policies.

8. Security

We take reasonable practical steps to handle information responsibly. However, no website, email service, messaging platform, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please share only information appropriate to your enquiry.

9. Your choices and privacy requests

You may reject optional Meta Pixel tracking or change your choice using “Cookie preferences”. You may also contact us to request that we correct or delete personal information you have sent directly to us, or to ask a question about how it is handled. We may need to retain some information where applicable law or legitimate recordkeeping needs require it.

Send a request to info@oravfx.com and describe your request. For your safety, do not include passwords or unnecessary sensitive information in the email. Rights and available remedies can depend on the law applicable to your situation.

10. Children's privacy

This website is intended to present ORA VFX's creative services and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us at the email below so we can review the request.

11. External websites

This website may link to third-party platforms, including Meta, WhatsApp, and social networks. When you follow an external link, the privacy practices of that service apply to your activity there. Review the relevant service's privacy information before sharing information with it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the website, our services, or applicable requirements. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates when this page was most recently revised. Please check this page periodically for changes.
